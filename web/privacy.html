Privacy Policy

Questions submitted through this service are processed by the central AI Engineering Knowledge Agent to retrieve relevant approved knowledge and generate an answer.

The service may store operational analytics needed for reliability, evaluation, and service improvement. Analytics can include channel, evidence status, confidence, latency, a deterministic question hash, and a short redacted question preview. The analytics table does not retain the full raw question.

User feedback may be stored together with the related request identifier.

Private source documents are not exposed through public clients. Public clients receive synthesized answers and source metadata.

The service runs on Cloudflare infrastructure and may use Workers AI, D1, and Vectorize.

User data is not sold.
